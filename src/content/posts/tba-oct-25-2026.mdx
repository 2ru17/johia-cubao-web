# A Heart To Serve

Happy Anniversary, JOHIA Cubao! As we look back at the years of God's faithfulness, we are reminded that this church is built not just on great preaching or programs, but on the sacrifices of individuals who carry a true heart to serve.

## The Heart of the House
When we serve the house of God, we are reflecting the very nature of Jesus, who came not to be served, but to serve. 

* **Serving with Purpose:** Every usher, creative, musician, and volunteer plays a vital role in the kingdom.
* **Serving with Joy:** True service doesn't stem from obligation, but from an overflow of our love for God.
* **Serving Together:** We are stronger when we build together. Our collective service is what makes this house a home.

## Application
As we step into a new year as a church family, ask yourself: How is God calling me to serve? Whether it's joining a new ministry team, leading a Life Group, or simply serving your family well this week—do it with the heart of Jesus. Here's to many more years of building together!
