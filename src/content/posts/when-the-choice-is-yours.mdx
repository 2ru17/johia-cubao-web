> "Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid or terrified because of them, for the LORD your God goes with you; he will never leave you nor forsake you." - Deuteronomy 31:6

We don't always get to choose the circumstances we face, but we always have the power to choose our response. When the pressure is on, our choices reveal where our true trust lies. 

## The Core Message
When you don't know what to do, choose who you seek:

* **Choose Your Focus:** The battle may be bigger than you, but it is never bigger than God. Where you fix your eyes determines your peace.
* **Choose Your Posture:** Will you respond in fear, or will you stand in faith? A posture of worship shifts the atmosphere of any battle.
* **God Goes With You:** You are not fighting alone. The ultimate choice is surrendering the outcome to the One who has already promised to never leave you.

## Application
Are you facing a difficult decision or an unexpected battle? Before you react, take a breath. Choose to respond with faith. Seek God's wisdom first, knowing He is walking right beside you.
