> "‘Not by might nor by power, but by my Spirit,’ says the Lord Almighty. ‘What are you, mighty mountain? Before Zerubbabel you will become level ground.’" - Zechariah 4:6-7

When returning to rebuild what was broken, Zerubbabel and the people faced overwhelming obstacles: scarce resources, fierce opposition, and a temple foundation that seemed impossibly vast. It is so easy in our personal lives—facing financial strain, health battles, family tensions, or community responsibilities—to believe that simply working longer, planning harder, or relying on our own proficiency will solve every problem.

Yet human energy eventually runs dry. The prophetic vision in Zechariah showed continuous, golden oil flowing freely from living olive trees into the lamps. God’s work does not rely on our self-sufficiency; it is fueled by the continuous, supernatural empowering of His Holy Spirit.

## The Core Message
When mountains rise before you, shifting your dependence changes everything:

* **From Human Striving to Holy Spirit Supply:** The question is never how capable or strong you are in your own flesh, but how reliant you are on God. Where human might ends, the Spirit's power begins.
* **Mountains Becoming Level Ground:** Challenges that appear insurmountable to human eyes become level ground before the Lord. God does not dismiss our trials; He dismantles them as we walk forward in simple obedience.
* **Faithfulness Over Perfection:** The Lord does not demand flawless perfection; He looks for a heart that remains available, humble, and attentive. Fruitfulness flows from nearness to Jesus, not frantic effort.

## Application
Take a moment to name the mountain in front of you today. Breathe out your striving and whisper this truth: *Not by might, nor by power, but by Your Spirit, Lord.* Allow His peace to quiet your anxiety and guide your next step.
