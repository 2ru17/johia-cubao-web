# The Jesus You Think You Know

We have spent months seeing what Jesus can do. Now, let’s slow down and see *who* Jesus is. Mark 5 shows us a Jesus who is not distant from human pain. He stops, listens, sees, and enters people’s suffering. He is not just concerned with the problem—He is concerned with the *person*.

## More Than an Interruption
Jesus doesn’t merely solve problems; He restores dignity. He sees the woman behind the crowd and Jairus behind the crisis. 

* **People matter:** To Jesus, the hurting are never an interruption. They are the focus.
* **Fully human, fully present:** He understands weakness, fear, grief, exhaustion, and rejection. 
* **Fresh eyes:** Familiarity can make us stop seeing what is beautiful about Jesus. We don't need a new Jesus; we need fresh eyes.

## Application
When was the last time you felt like an interruption? Remember today that Jesus sees you clearly in the middle of your crowd. Stop and see Him differently this week—not just as a problem solver, but as a compassionate Savior who enters your mess.
