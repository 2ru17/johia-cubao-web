# The Jesus Who Calls You

Jesus meets us in ordinary life. Peter was working, tired, and disappointed with a night of empty nets when Jesus stepped into his boat. You don't need a perfect or extraordinary life before Jesus calls you. He meets you exactly where you are.

## Turning the Empty into Purpose
Jesus can turn an empty season into a divine calling. 

* **He sees the future:** Where Peter saw empty nets, Jesus saw a future disciple. Your failure does not disqualify you from God's calling.
* **Trusting the Word:** "But because you say so, I will let down the nets." Following Jesus requires a response of faith, even when it doesn't make sense.
* **More than admiration:** It’s not enough to be amazed by Jesus. At some point, we have to drop our nets and follow Him.

## Application
Are you staring at an "empty net" in your life right now—a disappointment or a failed expectation? What is Jesus asking you to do? This week, take a step of faith and respond to His call, trusting that He sees a purpose you can't yet see.
