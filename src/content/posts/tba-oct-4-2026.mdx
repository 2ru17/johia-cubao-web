# The Jesus Who Is Beautiful

The beauty of Jesus is seen in how He sees people. In Luke 7, Simon the Pharisee looked at the woman with the alabaster jar and saw only a sinner. Jesus looked at her and saw a person who needed grace. He didn't see the label; He saw the human heart.

## Moving Toward the Mess
Jesus is unafraid to enter messy situations. He doesn’t distance Himself from broken people—He moves toward them with truth and grace.

* **He restores dignity:** Jesus doesn't just forgive sins; He lifts our heads and restores our worth.
* **He breaks false beliefs:** Grace means dealing with sin, not ignoring it. You don't need to fix yourself before coming to Him.
* **Our response:** When we see Jesus clearly, our hearts change, and we begin to see people the way He sees them.

## Application
Are you defining yourself—or someone else—by their past? This week, challenge yourself to look at others through the lens of grace. Remember that whoever has been forgiven much, loves much.
