> "Before I formed you in the womb I knew you, before you were born I set you apart; I appointed you as a prophet to the nations. 'Ah, Sovereign Lord,' I said, 'I do not know how to speak; I am too young.' But the Lord said to me, 'Do not say, I am too young.' You must go to everyone I send you to and say whatever I command you." - Jeremiah 1:5-7

When God calls someone into a deeper assignment, our most immediate human reaction is often hesitation. We tally up our perceived inadequacies: *I am too young, I am too old, I lack theological training, I am not eloquent, my past is too messy.* Like Jeremiah, we look inward and conclude that we are not enough.

Yet God's divine calling has never been an appraisal of our natural talent. God does not call the qualified; He qualifies the called. Before you ever took your first breath or wrestled with a single insecurity, your Creator had already mapped out a redemptive purpose for your life.

## The Core Message
Stepping into God's purpose requires exchanging our excuses for His promises:

* **Identity Precedes Readiness:** Your value and mission were settled by God before the foundation of the world. You do not need to wait until you feel fully prepared to say yes; obedience is the vehicle through which God provides the equipping.
* **Stop Thinking Small:** It is easy to confine our expectations to small, comfortable boxes. But God's vision for your life stretches far beyond your comfort zone. He uses the weak, the overlooked, and the ordinary to display His extraordinary grace.
* **Willingness Over Polish:** God does not look for prestige or perfection; He treasures a heart that says, *“Here I am, Lord; send me.”* When God says He will go with you, His presence guarantees everything you will ever need.

## Application
Is there an area of service, leadership, or encouragement that you have been holding back from out of fear? Silence the voice of imposter syndrome today. Trust that the One who called you is faithful to guide every step of the journey.
