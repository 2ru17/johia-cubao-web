> "Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls." - Matthew 11:28-29

We live in a world that praises the hustle, but often we find ourselves carrying burdens that we were never meant to hold. The Great Exchange is not about working harder to earn God's favor; it's about surrendering our exhaustion for His enduring peace.

## The Core Message
When Jesus invites us to exchange our yoke for His, He is making a radical offer:

* **Surrender Your Weight:** We hold onto guilt, anxiety, and the need for control. Jesus asks us to trade those heavy bags for His grace.
* **A New Way of Walking:** A yoke is designed for two. When we take on His yoke, we are no longer pulling the weight of our lives alone. He carries the heavy side.
* **Finding True Rest:** True rest is not the absence of work; it is the presence of peace in the middle of the work.

## Application
What heavy burden have you been dragging around this past week? Today, intentionally pause and pray. Ask Jesus to take that weight and give you His peace in exchange. You were never meant to carry it all.
