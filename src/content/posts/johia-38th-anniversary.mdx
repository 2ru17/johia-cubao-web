> "Be strong and courageous, and do the work. Do not be afraid or discouraged, for the Lord God, my God, is with you." — 1 Chronicles 28:20 (NIV)

Since 1988, God has been writing an extraordinary story of faith, hope, and transformation through Jesus Our Hope International Assemblies. Sa loob ng 38 years, naranasan natin ang katapatan ng Diyos sa bawat season.

As we celebrate this milestone, we look back with deep gratitude, stand firm in our calling, and move forward with faith in the One who holds our future.

## Honoring the Past, Reaching for the Future

**We celebrate what God has done, but we refuse to settle for what has already been done.** Ang ating nakaraan ay patotoo ng kabutihan ng Diyos, ngunit hindi dito nagtatapos ang ating kuwento.

May mga buhay pang kailangang maabot, mga pusong kailangang mabago, at mga komunidad na kailangang mapuno ng pag-asa ng Diyos.

## Built for This: Doing the Work Together

As we embrace our 2026 theme, **“Do the Work Together, Be Strong,”** and our series declaration, **“Built for This,”** we are reminded that we are chosen for a purpose, strengthened by His presence, and called to build together.

* **Called to unity:** Hindi tayo tinawag upang gawin ang gawain nang mag-isa, kundi upang magkaisa, maglingkod, at maging tapat sa ipinagkatiwala ng Diyos.
* **Walking in His strength:** The work may not always be easy, and the road ahead may not always be clear, but we do not walk alone.

## The Heart of Everything: Jesus Christ

**At the heart of everything we do is Jesus Christ.** He is our foundation, our message, and our hope. We exist to love and honor God, raise Christ-committed followers, and live on purpose.

We long to see lives renewed, hearts transformed, families strengthened, and communities changed through the Gospel:

* **More than a gathering:** We believe the Church is not merely called to gather people, but to help people become more like Jesus.
* **Making disciples:** We are called not simply to fill seats, but to make disciples.
* **Multiplying a movement:** Not merely to maintain a legacy, but to multiply a movement of Christ-centered love, authentic transformation, and enduring hope.

## The Mission Continues

Thirty-eight years is not the end of our story—it is another reminder that **God is still at work**.

Ang Diyos na naging tapat sa ating nakaraan ay Siya ring Diyos na gagabay sa ating kinabukasan. The mission continues. The vision lives on. And together, by His grace, we will keep building what He has called us to build.

> **We are chosen. We are not alone. WE ARE BUILT FOR THIS!**

## Application

As we commemorate 38 years of God’s goodness, let us take action as one family:

* **Reflect with Gratitude:** Spend time thanking God for the leaders, families, and servants who faithfully laid the foundation of this house.
* **Commit to the Work:** Ask the Lord how you can actively contribute your gifts, time, and heart to the work God is doing in JOHIA today.
* **Multiply Hope:** Reach out and share God's transforming love with your family, friends, and community this week.
