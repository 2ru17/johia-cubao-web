> "Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, because it had its foundation on the rock." - Matthew 7:24-25

Storms in life are inevitable. The question is not *if* the winds will blow, but *what* your life is built upon when they do. This message reminds us that endurance isn't about avoiding the storm, but anchoring into the right foundation.

## The Core Message
How do we remain standing when everything around us is shaking?

* **Check Your Foundation:** Are you building on culture, opinions, or your own strength? Those will wash away. Only the Word of God remains forever.
* **The Process of Preparation:** We must anchor ourselves *before* the storm hits. Daily prayer and devotion build the resilience needed for tomorrow's trials.
* **God Sustains:** You don't have to hold yourself together. If your foundation is Christ, He is the one holding you.

## Application
Take an honest inventory of your heart this week. Where are you seeking security outside of Jesus? Reposition your trust, dig deep into His word, and know that He will keep you still standing.
