> "Enlarge the place of your tent, stretch your tent curtains wide, do not hold back; lengthen your cords, strengthen your stakes." - Isaiah 54:2

Many of us are living in spaces too small for the calling God has placed on our lives. A breakout requires us to stretch beyond our comfort zones and trust that God is expanding our capacity for His glory.

## The Core Message
Stepping into a breakout season requires faith and action:

* **Enlarge Your Vision:** Stop looking at your current limitations and start looking at God's infinite resources.
* **Do Not Hold Back:** Fear of failure often keeps us paralyzed. God commands us to stretch our faith and step into the unknown.
* **Strengthen Your Stakes:** As God expands your influence and territory, you must deepen your roots in Him. Growth without spiritual depth is dangerous.

## Application
What is one area of your life where you have been playing it safe? This week, take a bold step of faith. Enlarge your tent and prepare for God to move in ways you haven't seen before.
