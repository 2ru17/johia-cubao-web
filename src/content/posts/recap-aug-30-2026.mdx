God has called us to go beyond where we are, change our mindset, surround ourselves with excellence, and trust that God is our Wingman. 🙏

Don’t let your current situation define your future. See what God sees. Believe for more. Prepare for what He is calling you to.

> “Enlarge the place of your tent…” - Isaiah 54:2

It’s time to expand. It’s time to conquer. It’s time to see life through God’s eyes. 💛
