> "A furious squall came up, and the waves broke over the boat, so that it was nearly swamped. Jesus was in the stern, sleeping on a cushion. The disciples woke him and said to him, 'Teacher, don’t you care if we drown?' He got up, rebuked the wind and said to the waves, 'Quiet! Be still!' Then the wind died down and it was completely calm." — Mark 4:37–39

Life rarely follows our planned timeline. There are seasons when God allows an unexpected pause or a furious squall to break across our journey—not to destroy us, but to reveal His presence in a deeper way.

Sometimes Jesus interrupts our plans by inviting us into a storm we never anticipated. The disciples were directly following Jesus' instructions when the tempest arose. Encountering rough waters does not mean you chose the wrong direction; you can be exactly in the center of God's will and still face a storm.

## Pauses Can Be Painful — And That’s Okay

**The interruption often begins before we realize anything is amiss.** We stay busy paddling forward, yet beneath the surface, fatigue and vulnerability quietly build up.

* **God is present in the pause:** An unexpected delay is not a denial of His promise. Even when progress halts, God is actively at work in the quiet.
* **Silence is not absence:** Jesus was sleeping in the stern, yet His resting was never indifference. He remained right in the boat through every wave.
* **Restoring perspective:** God allows interruptions so we shift our attention away from human self-reliance and back toward His sovereignty.

> "As he went along, he saw a man blind from birth. His disciples asked him, 'Rabbi, who sinned, this man or his parents, that he was born blind?' 'Neither this man nor his parents sinned,' said Jesus, 'but this happened so that the works of God might be displayed in him.'" — John 9:1–3

## A Pause in Your Plan, But Not in His Purpose

**Real faith is revealed when the boat feels swamped.** It is effortless to declare trust in Jesus when the waters are tranquil, but the storms of life don't create our convictions—they expose where our trust truly lies.

* **Breaker of religious misconceptions:** Having Jesus in your boat does not guarantee storm-free seas. It guarantees an unsinkable anchor in the middle of them.
* **His authority over the elements:** With three words—*“Quiet! Be still!”*—the chaotic winds ceased. The circumstances that threaten to swamp you are completely subject to His voice.
* **His timing is intentional:** Jesus doesn’t always calm the waves immediately because He first wants to show you that His presence alone is sufficient.

## What's Next?

**Every divine interruption can become a springboard into the next chapter.** When your plans are altered, don’t spiral into frustration or despair. Turn to the Lord and ask what He wants you to see.

* **Ask God for the next step:** Rather than obsessing over lost momentum, seek His wisdom for the season directly ahead.
* **Anchor your confidence:** When you cannot decipher what tomorrow holds, cling tightly to the One holding tomorrow.
* **Rest in His faithfulness:** The storm may interrupt your personal itinerary, but it can never interrupt His redemptive presence.

## Application

What interruption, unexpected pause, or storm are you navigating this week? Instead of reacting out of fear or anxiety, pause and invite Jesus into that exact concern. Pray every day this week: *"Lord, even when I don't understand this storm, I thank You that You are right here in the boat with me."* Anchor your peace in His unchanging character.
