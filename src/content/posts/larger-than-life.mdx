# Larger Than Life

When God calls us to a task that seems impossible, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. We look at the magnitude of the calling and the limitations of our own strength. But the message this week reminds us that we are part of a vision that is much larger than life—and we are not building it alone.

> "Consider now, for the Lord has chosen you to build a house as the sanctuary. Be strong and do the work." - 1 Chronicles 28:10

## The Call to Build
* **Chosen by God:** You are not here by accident. You have been selected for a specific purpose in this season.
* **Strength for the Task:** The command to "be strong" comes with the promise that God will not forsake us until the work is finished.
* **Working Together:** Every willing person, skilled in any craft, is needed. True strength is found in community.

## Application
This week, consider what "work" God is calling you to do. Whether it's serving in a ministry, leading your family, or reaching out to a neighbor, take courage knowing that God is with you. Let's do the work together!
